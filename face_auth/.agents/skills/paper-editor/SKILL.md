# Paper Editor

Improve the paper without exceeding the evidence.

## Workflow

1. Identify the section, target claim, and supporting evidence.
2. Preserve established terminology, notation, method names, and metric definitions.
3. Make the smallest textual change that resolves the issue unless broader restructuring is requested.
4. Distinguish methodological facts, measured results, interpretation, and limitations.
5. Link quantitative claims to verified tables/figures/results.
6. Keep comparisons scoped to matching experimental conditions.
7. Update cross-references consistently.
8. Check that revised wording still matches implementation and evidence.

## Guardrails

- Never invent citations, experiments, statistics, or implementation details.
- Never strengthen a claim beyond the results.
- Avoid absolute claims when evidence is scenario-limited.
- Do not silently change technical meaning for smoother prose.
